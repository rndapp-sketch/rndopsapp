# Direct Purchase — Staff Manual

**Purpose of this document**: This is a simple, non-technical guide for staff. It explains what Direct Purchase is, who is involved at each step, what each person needs to do, and what happens next. No computer/system knowledge is required to follow this manual — it describes the process as it actually runs in the system today.

---

## 1. What is Direct Purchase?

Direct Purchase is the process used to buy items/equipment for a project, costing **up to ₹2.5 Lakh**, without going through a full tender process. The request passes through a fixed chain of approvals, then through verification and sanction steps, before a final Purchase Order is issued.

The journey has **3 broad parts**:

1. **Approval** — the request is approved by a chain of people, ending with either the Dean or (in some cases) the Director.
2. **Verification & Sanction** — RnD Staff verify the paperwork and generate the financial Sanction Sheet.
3. **Purchase Order** — the final order is generated and sent to the vendor.

### Amount Limits at a Glance

There are **three different money thresholds** used across this process — it's easy to confuse them, so here they are together:

| Amount / Threshold | What it triggers |
|---|---|
| **Up to ₹2,50,000** | The general ceiling for a request under this Direct Purchase module |
| **More than ₹2,00,000** | A **Purchase Committee** of at least 3 members becomes mandatory on the request (see Section 4) |
| **More than ₹3,00,000** — *only for Consumable / Contingency account heads* | The Dean forwards the request to the **Director** for an extra approval, instead of approving it directly (see Section 3) |

**Important note on the ₹3 Lakh threshold**: This Director-escalation rule applies **only** to purchases booked under the **Consumable** or **Contingency** budget heads. Because ₹3 Lakh is higher than the general ₹2.5 Lakh ceiling, it means Consumable/Contingency purchases can, in practice, go above ₹2.5 Lakh as long as they get the additional Director sign-off. If you are unsure whether your purchase's budget head qualifies for this extended limit, check with the RnD office before submitting.

## 2. Who is Involved (Roles)

| Role in this manual | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Project Staff** | Staff member working on a project who raises the purchase request |
| **PI (Project Investigator)** | Person in charge of the project. In the system this person holds the "Permanent Employee" role |
| **NPDF / Research Fellow** | Independent Researcher raising a request |
| **Mentor** | Guide/supervisor of an NPDF |
| **Inspire Faculty** | Inspire Faculty member raising a request |
| **HOD/HOC** | Head of Department / Head of Center — approves for Inspire Faculty |
| **RnD Staff** | Research & Development office staff who verify paperwork and process sanction/PO steps |
| **Head of Section (RnD)** | Approves after RnD Staff, before the request goes to the Dean |
| **Dean (RnD)** | Approves most requests; escalates high-value Consumable/Contingency requests to the Director |
| **Director** | Only involved for high-value Consumable/Contingency purchases (see Section 4) |

**Note**: Anyone holding the general **"RnD"** role can also step in and approve at the RnD Staff, Head of Section, or Dean stage as a backup — these three stages are not strictly limited to one named person each.

## 3. Who Starts the Request, and the Approval Path

The starting point depends on **who is raising the request**:

| Requester | Request first goes to |
|---|---|
| Project Staff | PI (Project Investigator) |
| PI raising their own request | Skips straight to RnD Staff |
| NPDF / Research Fellow | Mentor |
| Inspire Faculty | HOD/HOC |

After that first step, **every request follows the same common path**:

```
        Project Staff / PI / NPDF / Inspire Faculty
                  raises the request
                        │
                        ▼
        ┌───────────────────────────────────┐
        │  If raised by Project Staff        │  →  PI approves
        │  If raised by NPDF                 │  →  Mentor approves
        │  If raised by Inspire Faculty      │  →  HOD/HOC approves
        │  If raised by PI directly          │  →  (no extra step here)
        └───────────────────────────────────┘
                        │
                        ▼
                 RnD Staff approves
                        │
                        ▼
             Head of Section (RnD) approves
                        │
                        ▼
                  Dean (RnD) approves
                        │
        ┌───────────────┴────────────────┐
        │                                 │
   (most requests)              ONLY IF: Account Head is
        │                       "Consumable" or "Contingency"
        │                       AND amount is more than ₹3 Lakh
        │                                 │
        │                                 ▼
        │                        Director approves
        │                                 │
        ▼                                 ▼
              REQUEST APPROVED
```

**In short**: Almost every request is fully approved once the **Dean** signs off. The **Director** only needs to approve if the purchase is booked under **Consumable or Contingency** and the amount is **above ₹3 Lakh** — in that one case, the Dean forwards it to the Director instead of approving it directly.

## 4. What Each Person Checks

| Step | Who acts | What they check |
|---|---|---|
| Request creation | Project Staff / PI / NPDF / Inspire Faculty | Item list, quantity, estimated cost, and justification are filled correctly |
| First approval | PI / Mentor / HOD/HOC (as applicable) | Whether the purchase is genuine and needed for the project |
| Staff review | RnD Staff | Paperwork completeness, budget head correctness |
| Section review | Head of Section (RnD) | Overall correctness before Dean's sign-off |
| Final approval | Dean (RnD), or Director for high-value Consumable/Contingency cases | Final sign-off, financial responsibility |

**Special rule — Purchase Committee**: If the total estimated cost of items is **more than ₹2,00,000**, the requester must add a **Purchase Committee** of at least **3 members** at the time of filling the request. This is checked automatically before the request can be submitted.

## 5. After Approval — Verification, Sanction & Purchase Order

Once the request is **Approved**, it moves into a second phase, handled mainly by the **PI** and **RnD Staff**:

| Step | Who acts | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Submit P-11 | PI | PI submits the P-11 form (item details are carried over automatically — no retyping) |
| 2. Verify Hardcopy | RnD Staff | RnD Staff verifies the physical/hardcopy paperwork |
| 3. Generate Sanction Sheet | RnD Staff | RnD Staff prepares the Sanction Sheet, adding any "Other Charges" (e.g., freight, packing) on top of the item cost |
| 4. Mark Print Taken | PI | PI takes a physical print of the Sanction Sheet |
| 5. Verify Sanction Sheet | RnD Staff | RnD Staff does a final check and approves the Sanction Sheet |
| 6. Generate PO | RnD Staff | The final Purchase Order is generated and sent to the vendor |

**Note on amounts**: The final Purchase Order value includes only the **item cost + packing/forwarding + freight**. "Other Charges" recorded in the Sanction Sheet are handled separately and are **not** part of the PO amount.

## 6. Quick Reference — Full Status Flow

The full journey is easiest to follow in **two parts**: first the internal **Approval Chain**, then the **Post-Approval Chain** (verification, sanction, and PO). Each arrow is labelled with the action button and who presses it.

### Part A — Approval Chain (Draft → Approved)

```
                              Draft
                                │
                            ( Submit )
                                │
                                ▼
        Pending PI / Mentor / Head / Staff Approval
        (which one, depends on who raised the request — see Section 3)
                                │
                            ( Forward )
                                │
                                ▼
                    Pending Staff Approval
                      handled by RnD Staff
                                │
                            ( Forward )
                                │
                                ▼
                    Pending HoS Approval
                 handled by Head of Section (RnD)
                                │
                            ( Forward )
                                │
                                ▼
                    Pending Dean Approval
                      handled by Dean (RnD)
                                │
                ┌───────────────┴────────────────┐
                │                                 │
          ( Approve )                        ( Forward )
        Almost all requests            Only if: Consumable/Contingency
                                        AND amount is more than ₹3 Lakh
                │                                 │
                ▼                                 ▼
           ✅ APPROVED  ◄──────────────  Pending Director Approval
                              ( Approve )          │
                                          handled by the Director
```

**In plain words**: The request climbs a fixed ladder — RnD Staff → Head of Section → Dean — and almost always ends there with the Dean's approval. The only detour is for **Consumable/Contingency** purchases above **₹3 Lakh**, where the Dean forwards it one level higher to the **Director** for the final sign-off instead.

---

### Part B — Post-Approval Chain (Approved → Purchase Order)

```
                          ✅ APPROVED
                                │
                    ( Submit P-11 — by PI )
                                │
                                ▼
                Pending Staff Verification
                                │
                ( Verify Hardcopy — RnD Staff )
                                │
                                ▼
                       RDP-11 Verified
                                │
            ( Generate Sanction Sheet — RnD Staff )
                                │
                                ▼
                Sanction Sheet Generated
                                │
                ( Mark Print Taken — by PI )
                                │
                                ▼
                 Sanction Sheet Printed
                                │
            ( Verify Sanction Sheet — RnD Staff )
                                │
                                ▼
                    Sanction Approved
                                │
                  ( Generate PO — RnD Staff )
                                │
                                ▼
                       🏁 POGenerated
              (Final stage — Purchase Order is ready)
```

**In plain words**: Once Approved, the **PI** and **RnD Staff** take turns — PI submits the P-11 and later takes the Sanction Sheet print; RnD Staff verifies paperwork, generates the Sanction Sheet, verifies it, and finally generates the Purchase Order. No one else needs to act during this second half of the journey.

**If rejected**: At any approval step in Part A (or, where applicable, Part B), the current approver may instead **reject** the request. It then moves to a **Rejected** status and is closed permanently — the requester must raise a brand-new request if the purchase is still needed.

## 8. What Happens When Each Action Button Is Clicked

This section explains, button by button, exactly what happens on screen and behind the scenes.

### Submit *(Draft → first approval stage)*
- **Who clicks it**: Whoever raised the request (Project Staff / PI / NPDF / Inspire Faculty).
- **What happens**: The draft is locked from further editing by the requester. The request leaves "Draft" and lands in the first approver's pending list — PI, Mentor, HOD/HOC, or RnD Staff, depending on who submitted it. The requester cannot edit it again unless it is sent back.

### Forward *(PI / Mentor / Head approval → Staff Approval, and Staff Approval → HoS Approval → Dean Approval)*
- **Who clicks it**: The current approver (PI, Mentor, HOD/HOC, RnD Staff, or Head of Section).
- **What happens**: No data changes. The request simply moves ahead to the next person's pending list. Clicking Forward means "I approve this from my side — send it to the next person."

### Approve *(Dean stage — most requests)*
- **Who clicks it**: Dean, or anyone with the general RnD role.
- **What happens**: If the account head is **not** Consumable/Contingency, or it is but the amount is **₹3 Lakh or less**, clicking Approve takes the request straight to **"Approved"** — the final internal sign-off. At this point, the system automatically records the fund commitment against the project's budget in the background; no separate action is needed from anyone. The requester and PI are notified that the request is now Approved.

### Forward *(Dean → Director, only for Consumable/Contingency above ₹3 Lakh)*
- **Who clicks it**: Dean, or anyone with the general RnD role.
- **What happens**: Instead of approving directly, the Dean sends the request one level up to the Director for an extra sign-off — this only happens because of the account head + amount combination.

### Approve *(Director stage)*
- **Who clicks it**: Director.
- **What happens**: Final sign-off for the high-value Consumable/Contingency case. Moves the request to **"Approved"**, exactly like the Dean's direct approval path — this is the last internal approval step, and the budget commitment is recorded here.

### Submit P-11
- **Who clicks it**: PI.
- **What happens**: A new **P-11 Form** is automatically created and pre-filled with all item details, quantities, and prices from the original request — nothing needs to be retyped. The request moves to **"Pending Staff Verification"** and appears in the RnD Staff queue for hardcopy verification.

### Verify Hardcopy
- **Who clicks it**: RnD Staff.
- **What happens**: RnD Staff confirms that the physical/paper documents match what's recorded in the system. No new document is created — this simply moves the status to **"RDP-11 Verified"**, clearing the way for the Sanction Sheet to be generated.

### Generate Sanction Sheet
- **Who clicks it**: RnD Staff.
- **What happens**: A new **Sanction Sheet** document is automatically created, carrying over the item list from the P-11 form. RnD Staff can now add **"Other Charges"** (freight, packing, etc.) if applicable. Status becomes **"Sanction Sheet Generated"**.

### Mark Print Taken
- **Who clicks it**: PI.
- **What happens**: Confirms that the PI has physically printed the Sanction Sheet (for signatures/records). No financial figures change — it only records that the physical copy now exists. Status becomes **"Sanction Sheet Printed"**.

### Verify Sanction Sheet
- **Who clicks it**: RnD Staff.
- **What happens**: RnD Staff does a final check of the printed Sanction Sheet and confirms everything is in order. Status becomes **"Sanction Approved"** — the last checkpoint before the Purchase Order itself is generated.

### Generate PO
- **Who clicks it**: RnD Staff.
- **What happens**: The system calculates the final authorized Purchase Order value — **item cost + packing/forwarding + freight** (deliberately excluding the "Other Charges" added earlier). The request is then finalized and locked — no further edits are possible by anyone. Status becomes **"POGenerated"**. The Purchase Order can now be printed, sent to the vendor, and the signed copy uploaded and stored against this request.

### Reject *(available to whichever approver currently holds the request)*
- **Who clicks it**: The current approver at any approval stage (PI, Mentor, HOD/HOC, RnD Staff, Head of Section, Dean, or Director).
- **What happens**: The request is closed permanently and its status becomes **"Rejected"**. No further action is possible on it — a completely new request must be raised from scratch if the purchase is still needed.

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My request is stuck — who do I contact?**
A: For anything before "Approved," contact the person shown as the current approver in the flow above (PI, Mentor, HOD/HOC, or the RnD office). For anything after "Approved" (P-11, Sanction Sheet, PO), contact the RnD office, since they handle verification, sanction, and PO generation.

**Q: Do I need a Purchase Committee for every request?**
A: Only if the total estimated cost of items is **more than ₹2,00,000**. In that case, at least 3 committee members must be listed while filling the request.

**Q: Why did my request go to the Director instead of getting approved directly by the Dean?**
A: This only happens when the purchase is booked under **Consumable** or **Contingency** budget heads **and** the amount is **more than ₹3 Lakh**. In every other case, the Dean's approval is final.

**Q: Can I edit my request after submitting?**
A: Once submitted, a request cannot be edited directly by the requester. If something needs correction, it must be sent back by the current approver first.

**Q: What is the difference between the Sanction Sheet amount and the final Purchase Order amount?**
A: The Sanction Sheet may include "Other Charges" (for example, taxes not part of freight/packing) in addition to the item cost. The final Purchase Order reflects only the item cost plus packing/forwarding and freight — "Other Charges" are handled separately by RnD/accounts.

**Q: Who submits the P-11 form and takes the Sanction Sheet print?**
A: Both of these actions are done by the **PI**, even if the original request was raised by a Project Staff, NPDF, or Inspire Faculty member.

**Q: What happens if my request is rejected?**
A: The request is closed and moves to a **Rejected** status — it cannot be revived. A new request must be raised from scratch if the purchase is still needed.

**Q: I heard "RnD" staff can approve at more than one stage — is that a mistake?**
A: No. Along with the specific Staff / Head of Section / Dean approvers, anyone holding the general **RnD** role can act as a backup approver at any of those three stages. This is intentional, so approvals aren't blocked if a specific approver is unavailable.

### More FAQs — P-11, Sanction Sheet & Purchase Order Stage

**Q: What exactly is the P-11 Form, and why is it needed after the request is already Approved?**
A: The P-11 Form is a separate verification document created automatically once the Direct Purchase request is Approved. It carries over the same item list, quantities, and prices — the PI doesn't retype anything. Its purpose is to let RnD Staff verify the physical/hardcopy paperwork before any money is formally sanctioned, as a checkpoint separate from the initial approval chain.

**Q: What happens if RnD Staff finds a mismatch while doing "Verify Hardcopy"?**
A: RnD Staff will not click "Verify Hardcopy" until the paperwork matches. Instead, they will contact the PI/requester directly to correct or resubmit the physical documents. The request stays at "Pending Staff Verification" until this is resolved — no system status changes on its own.

**Q: What counts as "Other Charges" in the Sanction Sheet?**
A: These are additional costs beyond the basic item price that RnD Staff records at the Sanction Sheet stage — for example, taxes/duties or miscellaneous charges that aren't part of packing, forwarding, or freight. They are tracked for financial record-keeping but are deliberately left out of the final Purchase Order value.

**Q: What if the PI delays clicking "Mark Print Taken" — does the request just stay stuck?**
A: Yes, the request will remain at "Sanction Sheet Generated" until the PI takes the physical print and marks it. This step depends entirely on the PI's action, so delays here are usually a PI follow-up item, not an RnD office delay.

**Q: Can the Sanction Sheet be edited after RnD Staff generates it?**
A: The Sanction Sheet is generated once from the P-11 Form's data. Any correction needed after generation should be raised with RnD Staff directly, since there's no separate self-service edit step for the requester or PI at this stage.

**Q: Once the status is "POGenerated," can anything still be changed?**
A: No. "POGenerated" is the final, locked stage — the Direct Purchase request is formally submitted in the system at this point and cannot be edited further by anyone. Any further correction (e.g., a vendor-side change) would need to be handled outside this request, typically via a fresh process.

**Q: Where is the final Purchase Order document stored, and how do I get a copy later?**
A: Once generated, the signed PO document is uploaded and stored against this specific request (linked to the project and application ID). The RnD office can retrieve it on request — it does not need to be re-uploaded or re-created if you need a copy later.

**Q: Why does the amount shown at the P-11/Sanction Sheet stage sometimes look different from the original request amount?**
A: The original request has only the **estimated item cost**. The Sanction Sheet stage adds **Other Charges** (e.g., taxes) on top of that for financial tracking. The final **Purchase Order**, however, reverts to a narrower figure: item cost + packing/forwarding + freight only — so it's normal to see three slightly different totals across the three documents, each for a different purpose.
